# Outage Restoration SOP

When an outage is reported, customer operations should confirm affected premises, check OMS grouping, and avoid promising an exact restoration time until dispatch confirms field conditions.

Engineering triage should review last-gasp AMI alerts, feeder lockout telemetry, known vegetation exposure, and recent work orders on the affected circuit. Crew safety comes before restoration speed.

Customer-facing explanations should include the outage area, current status, whether a crew is assigned, and the next update window. Do not disclose precise crew location or critical infrastructure details.

Switching orders, backfeed decisions, and clearance releases require qualified operator approval and must not be autonomously executed by AI systems.
